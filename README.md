# Bookstore SQL Analysis Project

## Project Overview
This project analyzes bookstore sales data using PostgreSQL. The database includes three tables: Books, Customers, and Orders.

The goal of this project is to practice SQL for business analysis by answering questions about revenue, customers, orders, book popularity, genre sales, author sales, and remaining stock.

## Business Questions Answered
- Retrieved books by genre and publication year
- Found customers by country
- Analyzed orders by date and total amount
- Calculated total stock and total revenue
- Found the most expensive book and lowest-stock book
- Calculated total books sold by genre
- Found customers with at least 2 orders
- Identified the most frequently ordered book
- Listed top 3 most expensive Fantasy books
- Calculated total quantity sold by each author
- Found the customer who spent the most
- Calculated remaining stock after fulfilling orders
